How to use this. Every title links straight to the posting. On each scan the page behind every link is opened and read: Link checked means it loaded and still reads as open, May be closed means the page itself says the job is filled or expired, and Unverified means the site blocked the check — common on the big boards and usually not a problem. Anything that 404s or says it's closed on two scans running is removed.

Multiple rows for the same job title at one employer are intentional — those are separate openings, not duplicates.

Green rows are new since the last scan, and they group at the top. The shading clears the moment you tick Applied, so anything still green is something you haven't dealt with yet.

Dates. "Posted" is the employer's own date where the listing states one. "Seen" means it didn't, so that's the date this board first picked it up — a floor on its age, not its real age. Either way, newest sits at the top; switch to Closest to sort by distance instead.

Sources. The last tag on each row says where the link goes. Anything marked (direct) is the employer's own careers page — those stay live longer and usually mean a faster application than going through a board.

On timing. Brokers fill entry-level roles in 2–6 weeks, so applying late October through November is what produces a January start. Roles marked verify start may begin mid-2027.

Biggest lever. A direct note to the CAC supervisor beats any portal. Second biggest: an Alabama P&C license before January.

Tracking. Checkmarks and hidden rows save in whatever browser you're using and stay there between visits. They're private to that browser — they don't sync across devices and nobody else sees them. New scans keep your checkmarks; a listing stays checked even if its link changes.
